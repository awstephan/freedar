# Freedar v0.1.2

A cleaner full-screen radar view and easier panel dismissal. Requires **Android 8.0 or newer**.

## What's new

- Controls now hide after five seconds of inactivity, including the timeline and
  frame-time label. Radar playback continues in the full-screen view.
- Touch the map, move the pointer or use the keyboard to show controls again.
- **Settings → Auto-hide controls** lets you keep controls visible instead. Your
  choice is remembered across app restarts; auto-hide is enabled by default.
- Open panels, active interaction, keyboard focus within controls, locating and
  accessibility touch exploration keep controls visible. Afterward, a fresh
  five-second countdown begins.
- Tap or start a map gesture outside an open panel to dismiss it. The tap or
  gesture still performs its normal action. Panel buttons still toggle their
  panels and switch directly between them.

## Install or update

Download [freedar-0.1.2.apk](https://github.com/awstephan/freedar/releases/download/v0.1.2/freedar-0.1.2.apk)
and install it over an earlier signed release to retain settings and cached data.
The universal APK supports ARM and x86 devices. `SHA256SUMS` contains its SHA-256 checksum.
Version code: **3**.

Debug installations use a different signing certificate. See the
[installation instructions](https://github.com/awstephan/freedar#download-and-install)
if switching from a debug build.
